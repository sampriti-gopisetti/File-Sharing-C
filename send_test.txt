If you are reading this, the bytes made it.

They left one program, were split into packets, numbered, and sent out across
the network. Some may have taken different paths, arrived out of order, or been
resent after getting lost along the way. TCP quietly put them all back in order,
and the receiver stitched them together until this message was whole again.

A small thing — a file moving from one place to another — but underneath it is
sockets, buffers, byte order, and a little agreed-upon protocol, all doing their
jobs so that a sentence written in one place can be read, unchanged, in another.

Built from scratch, understood end to end. Nicely done.
